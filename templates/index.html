How Gulf Disruption Radar Works

1. Real-Time Air Corridor Monitoring: Our system continuously tracks high-priority flights and airspace routes originating from or connecting through key GCC hubs (DXB, RUH, etc.) via AeroDataBox APIs.

2. State-Change Push Alerts: Instead of flooding your screen, our automated engine sends instant push notifications and Telegram alerts the moment a flight status flips to Canceled or Delayed.

3. PWA Installation & Notifications: By installing this web app to your phone or desktop home screen via PWA, you enable native background push capabilities to protect your supply chain in real time.
